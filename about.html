About Tiny Guitars

Our Story, Mission, and Passion

Our Story

Tiny Guitars was founded in London with a simple mission: to bring the joy and beauty of guitars to everyone, regardless of space constraints or budget. What started as a hobby project in a small workshop has grown into a beloved destination for tiny guitar enthusiasts worldwide.

Our founder, a lifelong guitar enthusiast and miniature craftsperson, realized that there was a gap in the market for high-quality, meticulously crafted miniature guitars that weren't just decorative pieces, but actual playable instruments.

Our Mission

We believe that music should be accessible and enjoyable in all forms and sizes. Our mission is to create the finest tiny guitars that combine:

  • Exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail
  • Authentic playability despite their miniature size
  • Affordable pricing for collectors and enthusiasts
  • Sustainable and ethical sourcing of materials
  • A commitment to customer satisfaction
