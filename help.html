Frequently Asked Questions

Tiny guitars offer a unique combination of musical artistry and collectible charm. They're perfect for music lovers with limited space, collectors who appreciate fine craftsmanship, and anyone who finds joy in miniature versions of beloved instruments. They also make fantastic gifts, desk decorations, and conversation starters. Despite their small size, many tiny guitars are actually playable, offering a genuine musical experience in a compact form.

We use a variety of high-quality materials to ensure authenticity and durability:

  • Woods: Spruce, mahogany, rosewood, maple, and exotic woods like koa and ebony
  • Strings: Nylon or steel strings (depending on model), properly scaled for miniature size
  • Hardware: Brass or chrome tuning pegs, metal frets, and authentic bridge components
  • Finishes: Natural lacquer, gloss, or matte finishes to protect and enhance the wood
  • Inlays: Mother-of-pearl, abalone, or synthetic alternatives for fretboard markers

All materials are ethically sourced and sustainably harvested where possible. We prioritize quality and environmental responsibility in every piece we create.

Proper care ensures your tiny guitar lasts for years:

  • Keep away from direct sunlight and extreme temperatures
  • Store in the provided case when not on display
  • Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth
  • If playable, check string tension periodically and retune as needed
  • Avoid exposing to moisture or humid environments
  • Handle with clean, dry hands to prevent oils from damaging the finish

With proper care, your tiny guitar will remain a cherished piece for generations.
